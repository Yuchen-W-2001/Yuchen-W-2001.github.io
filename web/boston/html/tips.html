Practical Tips for International Students in Boston

Moving to a new country can be exciting but challenging. This table provides clear and quick guidance for common tasks such as getting a license, opening a bank account, housing, transportation, and essential apps.

Student Life Tips Overview
Category Details
🚗 Getting a Driver’s License • Use your foreign license up to 1 year
• Bring passport, visa, I-20/DS-2019
• Provide proof of address
• Tests required: knowledge, vision, road test
• More info: RMV Website
🏦 Opening a Bank Account • Bring passport + I-20 + proof of address
• Initial deposit: $25–$50
• Ask for a student checking account
• Major banks: BOA, Chase, TD Bank
🏠 Renting an Apartment • Start searching 1–2 months early
• Sites: Zillow, Apartments.com, BostonPads
• Check lease length, deposits, utilities
• Always visit or video tour to avoid scams
🚆 Transportation Tips • Get a CharlieCard for cheaper fares
• Use MBTA app for real-time updates
• Boston is walkable—explore on foot
📱 Useful Apps & Resources • Google Maps (navigation)
• Transit App (MBTA tracking)
• Venmo / Zelle (money transfer)
• Facebook Marketplace (housing & furniture)
